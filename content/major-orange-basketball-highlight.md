## Locked recipe (brand direction)

- **Cadence:** hand-drawn anime on twos (~12fps), held key poses, smear +
  white emphasis frames — explicitly "NOT smooth AI interpolation." Kills the
  uncanny liquid-morph tell.
- **Camera:** static, decisive framing, hard cuts between distinct locked
  compositions (low hero angle, ECU on eyes, side-profile). No drifting.
- **Backgrounds:** constant anime speed-line / radial-burst rush; subject
  held crisp while the background streaks past.
- **Emotion:** genuinely happy, positive, showman — expression shifting shot
  to shot (grin → mid-air awe → beaming celebration), never a frozen smile.
- **Music:** uplifting modern synthwave (warm arpeggios, bright pads,
  feel-good groove). No 80s/90s montage sound. No vocals, no dialogue.
- **Content filter:** frame all action as sport/effort; combat verbs
  ("punch", "power up") trip the NSFW filter.
